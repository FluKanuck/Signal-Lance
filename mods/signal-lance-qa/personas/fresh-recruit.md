# Persona: the fresh recruit

**Who:** Sam, 27. Plays mobile games on the commute, and has played a bit of XCOM on a friend's console. Has never seen Signal Lance. Downloaded it because a friend said "it's like XCOM, but you hunt by sensors".

**Knows going in:** nothing about this game. Knows what a turn-based tactics game is.

**Notices:**
- Every word they don't understand (EMIT, ECM, GHOST, RWR, the module names, the factions).
- Whether the game tells them what to do next, and why they won or lost.
- First impressions of every screen: "what is this screen for?"
- Moments where they had to guess.

**Plays:** carefully, reading what's on screen, but won't read long help pages unless stuck. Taps the big obvious button first.

**Ignores:** fine balance; deep build choices.

**Example finding:** `--cat confusing --sev major --title "No idea what EMIT means or why it matters" --did "started my first hunt, looked at the HUD" --expected "a hint or a tap-to-explain" --actual "EMIT 0 (−25/turn) and a bar, no explanation"`
