Design Bureau

You set performance targets. The suit gets its mass, power and heat from these targets.

DESIGN REPORT
NOTEQuiet 4 is past the knee. The last step uses 3 lp of baffles.
NOTEFaceted scheme. The rear arc has no plate and full radar return.
NOTECramped cockpit. Operator fatigue increases by 1 for each hunt.
RULES
1
Targets set the suit. You set pace, endurance, quiet and a protection scheme. The bureau calculates servo mass, reactor size and heat at rest. Modules use the load that remains.
2
Each target has a knee. The last step of each target costs two times the mass or heat. A maximum value in one target takes load from all other parts of the suit.
3
The scheme changes the damage rules. Distributed uses the current rules. Vault puts heavy plate on the core and mast, and no plate on the limbs. Faceted gives the front arc strong plate and low radar return, but the rear arc has no protection. Shed is a bolt-on shell that you can drop during a hunt.
4
A compromise gives load now and adds a cost later. Cramped cockpit: +2 lp, operator fatigue +1. Exposed bus: +1 hardpoint, more EF emission, shock hits cause two times the damage. Hot-swap mounts: you can change a module during a hunt, MAG visibility +1. Overtuned servos: pace +1, servos wear one step faster.
5
Each design has a lineage. A Mk.II that stays within ±10% of the Mk.I costs less and needs less dock time. A new scheme needs a new design. A refit cannot change the scheme.
SAME FRAME, TWO DESIGNS
Nightjar
PACE 2 · QUIET 4 · FACETED · CRAMPED
Low signature from the front. Weak against attacks from the side or rear. The operator gets tired on a long contract.
Doorman
PACE 1 · QUIET 1 · VAULT · HOT-SWAP
The core is very hard to destroy. The suit loses its weapons early. It fits new weapons from the back rack during the hunt.
WHAT IT CHANGES
One choice changes mass, heat, damage and the campaign. The ship can use the same bureau.
RISKS
The hangar must show the result of each change immediately. Without this display, the bureau is only a spreadsheet.
BUILD SIZE · M · mostly hangar rules · replaces the flat rated and max load

The thick coloured edges are ports. The two heat sinks point at the reactor. The core is cold from the front and sends heat out of the rear.
RULES
1
Each location is a small grid. A grid is 2×2 to 3×3 cells, with approximately 20 cells for each suit. A module uses 1, 2 or 3 cells. One tap turns the module. The frame sets the grid shapes, not the module types.
2
Ports connect to adjacent modules. A heat sink that points at the reactor removes reactor IR. A cold processor adjacent to a radar decreases radar EM. Two emitters with a shared edge use one antenna. They weigh less, but they emit at the same time.
3
The spine supplies power and data from the CORE. Each step away from the CORE increases draw by a small quantity. When you lose a location, all locations after it on the spine stop. On some frames, BACK supplies MAST. On these frames, an attack from the rear can remove your radar.
4
The position sets exposure. Modules on BACK show to the rear arc. Modules on MAST show above cover. Emitters on the arms point in the direction that you aim. The hangar shows signature for each arc.
5
Heat moves to adjacent cells. Hot modules in a tight group cause wear on adjacent modules. Hot modules with space between them need more cells.
SAME FRAME, TWO LAYOUTS
Ember
TIGHT CORE · SINKS AT REAR
Cold front, hot and bright rear. Strong when it moves forward. It must not let an enemy get behind it.
Spread
EMITTERS ON ARMS · SENSORS ON TWO LOCATIONS
A mast hit does not remove all sensors. When the arms turn, the EM cone also turns across the street.
WHAT IT CHANGES
Each module gets a shape and a port. This adds options without new numbers. Facing becomes part of the build. The R12 hit locations become more important.
RISKS
The full grid needs space. The iPad and desktop hangar has this space. The phone test build can use a simple view: current slots, ports and arcs.
BUILD SIZE · L full grid / M simple view · hangar layout, spine and arc signature in the sim

Hosts & Plugs

Hosts are the main modules. Plugs change how a host operates. A chain connects hosts into one action.

RULES
1
Hosts do the work. There are approximately 40 host rows: sensors, emitters, weapons, movement modules and drones. Hardpoints hold hosts. Each host has 1 to 3 plug sockets.
2
A plug changes the behaviour of a host. Narrowband: the cone is one third, range +40%. Delayed: the host operates at the end of the turn. Burst: the host uses all its EN at one time for two times the effect, once in each hunt. Echo: the host operates again in the next turn at half effect. Cold: no heat, half rate. Relay: the host operates from a drone or an ally. Tagged: the host puts a beacon on the target.
3
Each plug can go on one host only. If Narrowband is on the radar, it cannot also be on the jammer. You decide where each plug goes.
4
A chain is one action. A chain connects up to three hosts with triggers: on fix, on sound, on radar lock. Each link adds its signature and one step of delay. A long chain is strong and loud.
5
Most loot is plugs. Salvage, faction stock and contract rewards are mostly plugs. New content goes into the plug drawer, not into the slot list.
SAME HOST, TWO PLUG SETS
Searchlight
LAMP · NARROWBAND · DELAYED
A narrow radar beam. It pulses at the end of the turn, after the suit moves.
Puppeteer
LAMP · RELAY · COLD
The radar pulses from the tether drone at half range. The enemy finds the drone, not the suit.
WHAT IT CHANGES
40 hosts and 60 plugs give more combinations than 250 separate modules. The 12 current location mods become plugs. Each host shows as one card with sockets.
RISKS
Each plug must describe an action, not a percentage. The runner must test chains for invalid combinations before release.
BUILD SIZE · M · mostly rows, plus a chain resolver in the sim
CONCEPT 04 · TAP THE TABS

Postures & Reflexes

You install more modules than the reactor can supply. A posture sets which modules get power.

FTL SEA POWER EMCON COMMAND: MODERN OPS FFXII GAMBITS
Lantern · Lighthouse
FITTED 18 / OUTPUT 14
SIGNATURE IN THIS POSTURE
REFLEXES · 3 OF 3 SLOTS · SLOTS COME FROM THE OPERATOR
IF painted by radar
THEN mask and change to DARK
IF jammed AND an ally has a contact
THEN change to LOUD
IF heard within 4 tiles
THEN stop and keep AP
RULES
1
Install more than the reactor can supply. The idle draw can be up to approximately 150% of reactor output. You cannot operate all modules at the same time. The hangar does not stop you at zero regen.
2
Set three postures. Each posture sets which modules get power and which emitters are on. The default postures are DARK, HUNT and LOUD. You can change the names for each suit.
3
A posture change costs time. A change at the start of your turn is free. A change during your turn costs 1 AP. When you leave DARK, your IR increases for one turn.
4
Reflexes operate during the enemy turn. Each suit has 2 to 4 IF/THEN rules. They operate like the R17 interrupt, but you set them in the hangar. The operator sets the number of slots and the conditions.
5
Doctrine applies to the full lance. Set the posture and reflexes for the lance one time. Then change them for each suit as necessary.
WHAT IT CHANGES
The build continues to have an effect after the drop. Emission control becomes a plan that you make in the hangar. This replaces the current radarOn, mask and stealth toggles.
RISKS
Three postures for four suits is a lot of setup. Templates must have good default postures. The log must show why each reflex operated.
BUILD SIZE · M · general toggles, plus a reflex check during the enemy turn
CONCEPT 05

Operator Sync

Some modules connect to the operator. The operator changes how the suit performs.

TRAIT
Cold Blood. Aim +20% when your IR is 2 or less.
SIGNATURE VERB · ONE TIME IN EACH HUNT
Mimic. Your EM shows as a field turret for 2 turns. Your sound is two times higher during this time.
BOND · WITH CPL. HARROW
Contacts go between you and Harrow with no EM emission. The range is 6 tiles, with line of sight.
SUIT SCAR
Worn left servo. Sound +1 when you move. Creep moves +1 tile.
RULES
1
Sync is the bandwidth of the operator. Sync is 4 to 8. Neural modules use Sync, not hardpoints: myomer boost, still-skin, neural fire-control and direct sensor feed. If you change the operator, the suit loses these modules.
2
Traits use the state of the suit. Cold Blood needs a cold reactor. Loud Mouth adds 3 to thumper range in LOUD. The best suit for a mission depends on the operator.
3
Each operator has one signature verb. A verb is an exception to one rule, and it has a counter. Examples: Mimic, Ghost Step, Burn-Through, Long Ear.
4
Operators can bond. Two operators who deploy together many times get a link. One suit can emit while its partner stays dark and receives the contacts.
5
Damage can leave a scar. A scar is a permanent quirk on a suit or an operator. Each scar has a small advantage. A damaged suit becomes a different suit, not only a weaker suit.
SAME SUIT, TWO OPERATORS
Kestrel
COLD BLOOD · MIMIC
Operates the Warden cold and fights from the dark. Needs a cold-burn reactor.
Brass
LOUD MOUTH · BURN-THROUGH
Operates the same Warden hot. Uses the thumper first and burns through jammers.
WHAT IT CHANGES
The operator becomes part of the build. This agrees with the exosuit reframe. It connects to the Gate 2 operator strand: skills, fragility and recruits.
RISKS
When an operator dies, the player also loses a build. This is correct for Standard difficulty. It is very hard on Ironman.
BUILD SIZE · M · needs the Gate 2 operators

Disciplines

Modules add points to six disciplines. Each breakpoint in a discipline gives a new behaviour.

2 ✓Passive bearings arrive on your turn, not on the next turn.
4 ✓Two passive channels on one contact give a fix. You do not need a fusion core.
6A contact that you heard keeps a trail for 2 turns after its sound stops.
MAKER SET · CORPORATE2 / 4 · ACTIVE
Corporate 2: sensor draw −1. Corporate 4: enemies need grade 3 to hear your LPI emitters.
RULES
1
There are six disciplines. GHOST: stay hidden. HOUND: passive sensors. WIRE: electronic warfare. LANTERN: active sensors. ANVIL: survive hits. HAMMER: cause damage. Each module adds 1 or 2 pips.
2
Breakpoints are at 2, 4 and 6. Each breakpoint gives a new behaviour, not a percentage. GHOST 4: if you do not move in a turn, the game removes your last sound. GHOST 6: one channel alone cannot give a fix on you.
3
Hardpoints can have a polarity. A module with the same polarity has half its weight. A module with a different polarity has 25% more weight. A polarity change needs dock time on the ship.
4
Makers have sets. Two or four modules from one maker give a set rule. Old Army 4: modules never arrive Failing, and field repair gets 1 more use. Foundry 4: reactive plate refills after each hunt.
5
The city learns. The campaign records the top discipline of your lance. Then the districts use counters. If you use GHOST often, the districts use more thermal sensors. If you use WIRE often, they use home-on-jam missiles.
SAME FRAME, TWO SPREADS
Phantom
GHOST 6 · HOUND 2
One channel cannot give a fix on it. It sees very little and needs a partner.
Listener
GHOST 3 · HOUND 5 · WIRE 2
It gets fixes without emission. Enemies hear it when it moves fast.
WHAT IT CHANGES
The hangar shows 250 rows as six bars. A breakpoint gives the suit an identity. The city counters make the player change builds during a campaign.
RISKS
Pips that do not reach a breakpoint can feel wasted. Make pips easy to move. The INTEL must show the city counters.
BUILD SIZE · S–M · a pip field on each row, plus 18 breakpoint rules
07 · RECOMMENDATION

The Stack

Each concept controls a different layer of the build. The layers can operate together.

01 BUREAU · LITETHE SHAPE
Targets, one scheme and some compromises
Replaces the flat rated and max load, and armour as extra hits.
02 SPINE · LITETHE BODY
Current locations, plus ports and signature by arc
Replaces the six flat bars. Use the full grid on iPad and desktop after the ports pass a test.
03 HOSTS & PLUGSTHE KIT · TEST FIRST
Hardpoints hold hosts. Most of the catalogue is plugs
Replaces the 12 location mods and most variant rows.
04 POSTURES & REFLEXESTHE HUNT · TEST FIRST
More modules than power, three postures, 2 to 4 reflexes
Replaces radarOn, mask and the stealth toggle.
05 OPERATOR SYNCTHE PILOT · GATE 2
Sync, traits, one verb, bonds and scars
A new layer. It needs the operators first.
06 DISCIPLINESTHE READOUT · GATE 3
Six bars for all layers above, and the city counters
It needs the full catalogue and the campaign.
WHY TEST 03 AND 04 FIRST
·
Both use parts that R18 built. Tags, mods and toggles are already rows and rules.
·
Both have an effect in the first hunt. The suit-building strand asks for this.
·
Plugs increase the number of possible builds. Postures keep the build important after the drop.
·
Test the bureau next. The ship also needs it.
